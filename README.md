# 🌌 Orbit Slingshot

**Orbit Slingshot** is a fast-paced, physics-based space survival game. Harness the gravity of procedurally generated planets to slingshot your rocket deeper into the cosmos, dodging deadly hazards and climbing the global leaderboards!

## 🚀 How to Play
* **Auto-Orbit:** Your rocket automatically captures into the gravitational pull of nearby planets.
* **Slingshot:** Click, tap, or press Spacebar to detach from orbit and launch yourself forward. 
* **Momentum Combos:** Time your releases perfectly to chain orbits and build massive combo multipliers for your score!

## ⚠️ Hazards & 💎 Power-ups
The universe is a dangerous place. As you fly further, you'll need to survive dynamic space hazards:
* **Asteroid Fields & Comets:** Collisions are instantly fatal! Thread the needle to rack up "Close Call" bonuses.
* **Solar Flares:** Navigate through blazing heat zones.
* **5 Unique Power-ups:** Collect energy across the galaxy to trigger **Shields**, attract stars with **Magnets**, warp forward through **Wormholes**, slow down reality with **Time Dilation**, or clear the screen with a massive **Gravity Pulse**.

## 🏆 Global Leaderboards & Progression
* **Compete Worldwide:** At the end of your run, submit your score to our live global leaderboard and see how you stack up against pilots worldwide.
* **Lifetime Stats:** Track your total distance flown, power-ups collected, comets dodged, and highest combo multipliers.
* **Unlockable Themes:** The further you fly, the more vibrant galactic backgrounds you will discover and unlock.

## ✨ Features
* **Procedural Universe:** Endless, procedurally generated planets (Earths, Gas Giants, Lava, Ice) and cosmic environments.
* **Dynamic Physics Engine:** Smooth orbital mechanics, drifting asteroids, and responsive collision detection.
* **Premium UI:** Beautiful glassmorphic design, immersive particle effects, haptic feedback, and dynamic audio scaling based on your combo multiplier.

Fly far, explorer!
